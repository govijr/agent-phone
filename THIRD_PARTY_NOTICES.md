## phone-harness

`native/mirror.m` and `src/mirror.rs` follow the iPhone Mirroring approach of
[phone-harness](https://github.com/phone-harness/phone-harness): finding the window by bundle ID,
detecting interstitials through accessibility, the two-signal frontmost check, window capture,
Vision text recognition, holding modifier keys as real keys, and pasting text.
